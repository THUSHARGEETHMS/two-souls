Two Souls

A cooperative adventure. Neither soul can finish alone.

Ember · Player 1

A D move, W jump, F burn / fire bolt. Walks through lava and flame, burns vines, dies in water.

Tide · Player 2

← → move, ↑ jump, Enter douse / water bolt. Swims in water, douses flame locks, dies in lava.

Together

Hold plates for each other, stand on each other's heads, and both reach your doors. Solo: Tab swaps souls.
